What may be included

A structured search with clear deliverables

Every engagement is tailored to your objective. Typical components of Patagonia Land Hunter:

  • Search brief: objective, budget, size, access, connectivity, land use and timeline
  • Target areas in Los Lagos and/or Patagonia—plus criteria to rule out mismatched zones
  • Active search across public listings, local networks and off-market contacts
  • Curated shortlist: a focused set of strong options, not a mass listing catalog
  • Preliminary field review: access, surroundings, connectivity, early signals and key questions before you make an offer
  • Site-visit coordination and joint tours when appropriate
  • Support comparing options and preparing to negotiate
  • Coordination with lawyers, brokers and technical professionals—without replacing their reports

Navigating legal and practical complexity

Buying land in Chile involves zoning, subdivision rules, services, access and documentation that vary by municipality. We help you move forward in order: what to review, when, and with whom—before you commit capital.

LAND ADVISORS does not replace legal advice, title review, survey, environmental or engineering studies. When needed, we help you engage those services before closing.
