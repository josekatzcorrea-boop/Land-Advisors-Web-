Highest and best use: home, cabins, long-term hold or business

By José Hernán Katz C. · Land advisory · Land Advisors Chile

Published July 8, 2026 · Updated July 8, 2026 · 7 min read

Each attractive lot suits a specific set of projects. Highest and best use closes our rural land series: which use makes sense for that property and sector.

Fifth part of the series. We bring together appreciation, costs, regulation and connectivity to answer one question: which project fits this land?

Four common objectives

  • Family and quality of life
  • Cabins or tourism
  • Long-term hold
  • Commerce or services

Signals by land use

  • Home → reliable route, services, year-round livability
  • Cabins → tourism demand, covenants that allow lodging
  • Long-term hold → entry price vs. conservative exit
  • Commerce → traffic flow and permitted use under the PRC

Closing the series

Buying with clarity means reading appreciation, adding costs, verifying regulation, measuring connectivity and aligning land use. The strategy call structures next steps; Personalized Search identifies alternatives within this framework.

Educational content. Not a promise of returns.

Frequently asked questions

Can I build cabins on any rural land?

It depends on the property. The PRC, covenants and services can limit tourist lodging on a specific lot.

Which service fits after the series?

A strategy call (Diagnostic) if you are still defining a zone; Personalized Search for a curated shortlist; a potential study if you already have land in view.
