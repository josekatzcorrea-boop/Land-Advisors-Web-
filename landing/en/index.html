FAQ

What clients ask most

Do you sell land?

No. We are independent land advisors—not a brokerage or listing portal. We guide buyers, with no inventory of our own to sell.

What is the first step?

A strategy call (Diagnostic, 1 UF): we understand your objective and deliver zones and criteria before you begin visiting land.

Does this work if I live in Santiago?

Yes. Many clients live outside the region. We help you arrive with a map and priorities—so every visit is intentional.

What if the land I like is not a strong buy?

We provide clear guidance and keep searching with you. Our role is a sound purchase—not a fast one.

Where do you work?

Lago Llanquihue and Malalcahuello: Puerto Varas, Frutillar, Llanquihue, Ensenada, Puerto Octay, and rural land near those towns.
