From a use restriction to commercial potential

Puerto Varas

+50% vs. purchase price

By José Hernán Katz C. · Land advisory · Land Advisors Chile

Published July 8, 2026 · Updated July 8, 2026 · 6 min read

A property near Puerto Varas carried an explicit commercial use prohibition in its listing. The market set it aside; our read of access, growth, and zoning identified a different opportunity.

What the market saw

The buyer sought land with commercial potential on rural land near Puerto Varas. The listing showed a strong location, but the documentation included an explicit commercial use restriction—enough for most prospective buyers to set the property aside.

What we identified

We evaluated real connectivity rather than map distance, traffic on the corridor, the growth trajectory of the sector, and the property's zoning history. The restriction existed on paper, but the local context—access improvements, adjacent urbanization, and municipal investment near town—pointed to commercial potential the listing did not convey.

  • Connectivity and real travel times to town services
  • Urbanization dynamics in adjacent sectors
  • Zoning and planning rules applicable to the property
  • Comparable sales for land with a similar highest and best use

Acquisition strategy

With verifiable information, the buyer moved forward with a clear view of the regulatory risk and the path to lifting the restriction. The decision rested on the relationship between entry price, highest and best use, and negotiation margin—rather than on how the land looked in photos.

Result

Following the required process, the commercial use prohibition was lifted. Estimated value capture was approximately 50% above the purchase price.

What this means for buyers

A listing restriction can conceal an opportunity—provided zoning and local market conditions are evaluated before making an offer. The reverse also holds: land that appears unrestricted on paper can prove costly when site preparation and infrastructure costs or connectivity do not support the project.

Frequently asked questions

Can a commercial use restriction always be lifted?

Not always. The outcome depends on the property, the current planning instrument, and the applicable procedure—each case calls for regulatory verification before assuming a path to lifting the restriction.

Why did the market pass on this land?

A visible prohibition in a listing acts as an automatic filter. Few buyers go on to evaluate connectivity, town growth, and local comparable sales—the full assessment that revealed this opportunity.

Did Land Advisors sell this land?

No. We are an independent land advisory firm, not a brokerage or listing portal. We support the purchase decision with market intelligence and local expertise.
