Frequently asked questions

Do I need to engage search to receive the complimentary Diagnostic?

The Diagnostic is free during this offer, and search or any other service remains entirely optional.

What does the complimentary Diagnostic include?

A strategy call (online or in person), recommended zones for your objective, and a clear framework to evaluate land before you offer. Regular value: 1 UF.

When does Personalized Search at 3.5 UF apply?

Only if, after the Diagnostic, you decide you'd like us to search with you. We provide a clear quote; nothing is committed automatically.

Is the 3.5 UF credited if I buy land?

Yes. If you engage our Purchase Advisory (2.5% of purchase price), the 3.5 UF paid for Personalized Search is credited against that fee.

How long is the offer valid?

The complimentary Diagnostic and search at 3.5 UF apply to bookings confirmed through September 30, 2026, subject to availability.

Do you work with buyers based outside the south?

Yes. Many clients live in Santiago or northern Chile and buy in the Lago Llanquihue basin and Malalcahuello. The initial session can be online.

Are you a brokerage?

No. Land Advisors is independent land advisory. We help you acquire the right land with local expertise and market intelligence.
