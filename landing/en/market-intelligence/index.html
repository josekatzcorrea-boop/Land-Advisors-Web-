Frequently asked questions

What does market intelligence mean at Land Advisors?

It is an integrated evaluation of land, zoning, connectivity, local market, and land use potential—completed before we recommend a buy or a pass. Our role is to guide your decision with a clear framework, independent of any listing.

Where should I start if I am new to buying in the south?

Our guide to buying land in Southern Chile structures the process in six steps: use, budget, sectors, site preparation, infrastructure, and expert support. While your zone is still open, the Diagnostic (1 UF) is the usual first service.

Does this replace legal advice?

No. Our content supports education and land advisory. Before making an offer, review zoning and documents with a lawyer and qualified professionals.
